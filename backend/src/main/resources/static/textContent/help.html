Welcome to the game

Click "Start New Game" button to begin.
Drag tiles to swap them with their nearest adjacent tiles. Put them in the right order to build the image in the board.

The fewer attempts, the better result!

To upload your own image:

- Click Upload Image button
- Select image from your hard drive
- Submit by clicking 'Upload & Split' button.


To claim your test NFT token:

1. Make sure you have the MetaMask browser extension installed.
2. Collect at least 100 points awarded for solving puzzles.
3. Click the "Claim" button, which activates after collecting points.
4. If you're playing the game for the first time, you'll be asked to link your MetaMask wallet.
5. Once your wallet is linked, accept the reward.
6. You may need a minimum amount of Polygon tokens to complete the transaction. You can get them for free here.
